# Outgoing Types 
There are several supported outgoing types:
- Monthly expenditures broken into:
    - Rent 
    - Transport 
    - Subscriptions
    - Food 
    - Gym 
    - Phone 
    - Clothing 
    - Other Expenses 
- ISA Losses 
- Trading Losses 

## Monthly expenditures 
Usually able to split from bank statement directly and allows in depth tracking of expenditures. 

## ISA and Trading Losses 
Both support monthly updates to portfolios.
