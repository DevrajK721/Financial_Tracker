# Income Types 
There are several sources of income that are supported:
- Salaries 
- Additional non-employment income 
- ISA Gains 
- Trading Account Gains

## Salaries 
Salaries are considered multi-facetedly and follow UK law determining income tax, NI and pension contributions. It supports (on a monthly basis): 
- Income (Before Tax)
- Pension Contribution (Salary Sacrifice)
- One-Time Bonus 
- Income Tax 
- National Insurance 
- Student Loan Repayment 
- Taxable Income 
- Income (After Tax)

The relevant portions of the salary connect directly to accounts, whether that be debts, pensions or bank accounts. 

## Additional non-employment income 
Additional non-employment income is generally non-taxable and support transfers between accounts. 

## ISA Gains and Trading Account Gains 
Both support monthly updates to portfolios.
