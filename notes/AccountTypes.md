# Account Types 
There are several supported account types spanning:
- Bank accounts 
- High-interest savings accounts
- ISAs 
- Lifetime ISAs
- Trading accounts 
- Pensions 
- Debts 

These accounts also have a linkage property and transfers can be conducted between them. 

## Bank Accounts 
These are simple accounts for storing money without accruing any interest or dividends. Typically it is used as a "landing" account for salaries and other sources of income. 

This account supports basic income additions and transfers. 

## High-Interest Savings Accounts
These are savings accounts designed to earn interest while keeping money accessible. They support transfers and monthly snapshot-based interest tracking.

## ISAs 
These are accounts typically built for long-term savings and as such have variable interest rate month to month. This account supports transfers and interest additions (or losses) on a monthly basis.

## Lifetime ISAs
These are ISA accounts used for a first home or later-life saving. They support transfers, monthly snapshots, and growth tracking. Any government bonus or interest can be captured naturally through start/end snapshots.

## Trading accounts 
These are free trading accounts held on broker accounts such as Binance and Trading 212 and are typically less regulated, although gains are taxable if money is withdrawn from these accounts. 
This accounts supports transfers and monthly P\&L tracking. 

## Pensions 
These are accounts typically holding pensions until retirement age. They are inaccessible until age 65 as per current legislation but continue to accrue interest over time. This accounts supports transfers and monthly interest additions (or losses). 

## Debts 
These contain any remaining debts from student loans to personal debts and support constant and variable interest rates (updated daily, weekly, monthly or annually). They grow over time and can support transfers from other accounts or directly from expenses.
